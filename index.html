The Doko Story

Woven with
Roots & Soul

The "Doko" — a traditional Nepali woven bamboo basket — carries everything. Harvests, goods, memories, and meaning. We built this café around that same spirit: a place that holds community, flavor, and peace in every corner.

Born in Nepalgunj with the energy of youth and the warmth of tradition, Café Doke blends hand-brewed specialty coffee with Nepali street bites, curated lounges, and an atmosphere that feels like coming home — even the first time.
