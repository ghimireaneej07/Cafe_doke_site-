The Doko Story

Woven with
Roots & Soul.

The Doko — a traditional Nepali woven bamboo basket — carries everything: harvests, goods, memories, and meaning. We built this café around that same spirit: a place that holds community, flavor, and peace in every corner.

Born in Nepalgunj with the energy of youth and the warmth of tradition, Café Doke blends hand-brewed specialty coffee with Nepali street bites, curated lounges, and an atmosphere that feels like coming home — even the very first time.

Why Doke?

Crafted for Those
Who Appreciate.

We pay attention to the details others overlook — because you deserve more than just a cup of coffee.

☕

Fresh Specialty Coffee

Single-origin beans, freshly ground to order. Every shot pulled with precision for optimal extraction and flavour.

🌿

Nature Inspired Space

Bamboo textures, living plants, warm wood — an environment consciously designed to bring you calm and clarity.

🫶

Traditional Hospitality

Rooted in Nepali warmth. Every guest is treated like family — because that's the Doko way.

📶

Fast & Free WiFi

High-speed internet throughout, making Café Doke the ideal workspace for students, creators, and professionals.

🌳

Outdoor Seating

A beautiful outdoor garden and patio space, perfect for morning coffees, evening conversations, and groups.

🎵

Curated Atmosphere

Thoughtfully curated playlists, ambient lighting, and occasional live music evenings to elevate every visit.
